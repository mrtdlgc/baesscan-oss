# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

We want baes scan to be a useful, safe, and welcoming project for self-hosters, contributors, and people learning how DEX indexing works. The basics:

- Be respectful. Disagreement is fine; personal attacks, harassment, and discrimination are not.
- Stay on topic. Use issues and discussions for project work, not market promotion or token shilling.
- Do not weaponize the bot. Reports, screenshots, or alerts used to harass individuals, dox wallets, or coordinate attacks on people are not welcome here.
- Keep secrets out of public spaces. Never share live tokens, RPC keys, R2 credentials, or other people's wallet data in issues, PRs, or discussions.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, any official channel linked from the README) and to project-related conduct in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately by the same channel listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or block accounts in response to violations.
